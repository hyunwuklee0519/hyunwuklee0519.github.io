I am an Assistant Professor at the GIST Graduate School of Advanced Semiconductor Engineering [[COSMIC Lab]](https://thecosmiclab.github.io).

My research interests are in computer architecture, with a focus on hardware/software co-design for efficient AI systems:

- **Hardware-software co-design for AI**: domain-specific accelerators, hardware-aware algorithms, and neural network compression (quantization, sparsity/pruning)
- **On-device AI acceleration**: efficient execution of multimodal AI (VLA, LLM, diffusion models) on edge hardware
- **System architecture for AI**: processing-near-memory (PNM), CXL, KV cache optimization, and multi-GPU systems
- **Hardware microarchitecture**: bandwidth-efficient memory hierarchies, specialized execution units, and GPU register/memory systems

I received my Integrated M.S./Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (Computer Architecture) from Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Won Woo Ro [[Lab]](http://escal.yonsei.ac.kr). Prior to joining GIST, I worked as a System Architect at Samsung Electronics.

I am looking for motivated M.S./Ph.D. students, postdocs, and interns. If you are interested, please email me with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
