I am an Assistant Professor at the GIST Graduate School of Advanced Semiconductor Engineering [[COSMIC Lab]](https://thecosmiclab.github.io).

My research interests are in computer architecture, with a focus on hardware/software co-design for efficient AI systems, including quantization, KV cache optimization, memory system design, and neural network accelerators.

I received my Integrated M.S./Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (Computer Architecture) from Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Won Woo Ro [[Lab]](http://escal.yonsei.ac.kr). Prior to joining GIST, I worked as a System Architect at Samsung Electronics.

I am looking for motivated M.S./Ph.D. students, postdocs, and interns. If you are interested, please email me with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
